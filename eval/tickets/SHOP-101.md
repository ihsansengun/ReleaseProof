# SHOP-101 Loyalty points

Synthetic ticket for the demo shop. In week 2 this text lives in Jira and is
read from there; until then scenarios cite it by requirement id.

## Acceptance criteria

- REQ-1 New customers receive 100 welcome loyalty points on their first qualifying order.
- REQ-2 Customers see their loyalty balance on the Account Overview page.
- REQ-3 The cart displays the loyalty points that could be earned from the purchase.
- REQ-4 Changes to cart contents update the potential points.
- REQ-5 Loyalty transactions are associated with the customer and the order.
- REQ-6 A paid order earns 1 point for each whole currency unit of the order total.
